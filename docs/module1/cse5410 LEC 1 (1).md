### **Chapter 1. Introduction** 

- **Robots : first adopted by Asimov in 1941 through his short** 

- **science fiction story, Runaround.** 

- **Based on the Robotics Institute of America(RIA) definition: “ A robot is a reprogrammable multifunctional manipulator designed to move materials, parts, tools, or specialized devices through variable programmed motions for the performance of a variety of tasks.”** 

- **The engineering point of view: Robots are complex, versatile, ( intelligent ) devices that contain a mechanical structure, a sensory system, and an automatic control system on the results of research in mechanics, electric, electronics, automatic control, mathematics, and computer sciences.** 

2 



**_California State University, San Bernardino_ School of Computer Science and Engineering** 

- **Isaace Asimov proposed the four refined laws of “robotics” to potect us from intelligent generations of robots:** 

**- Law Zero: A robot may not injure humanity, or, through inaction, allow humanity to come to harm.** 

- **Law One: A robot may not injure a human being, or, through inaction, allow a human being to come to harm, unless this would violate a higher order law.** 

- **Law Two: A robot must obey orders given it by human beings,** 

- **except where such orders would conflict with a higher order law.** 

- **Law Three: A robot must protect its own existence as long as such protection does not conflict with a higher order law.** 3

The first programmable robot was designed by George Devol in 1954. - In 1959 the first commercially available robot appeared on the 1960 market. Robotic manipulators were used in industries after , and saw sky rocketing growth in 1980s.** 

- **Robots appeared as a result of combination two technologies: teleoperators, and computer numerical control (CNC) of milling machines.**

### **1.2. 5. End-effector** 

- **The end-effector is the part mounted on the last link to do the required** 

- **job of the robot.** 

- **The simplest end-effector is a gripper, which is usually capable of only** 

- **two actions: opening and closing.** 

- **The wrist and end-effector assembly is also called a hand.** 

- **1.2. 6. Actuators** 

- **Actuators are drivers acting as the muscles of robots to change their** 

- **configuration. The actuators provide power to act.** 

- **1.2. 7. Sensors** 

- **The elements used for detecting and collecting information about** 

- **internal and environmental states are sensors.** 

- **1.2.8. Controller (processor and software) 1. Information role, which consists of collecting and processing the infomation provided by the robot’s sensors** 

**2. Decision role, which consists of planning the geometric motion. 3. Communication role, which consists of organizing the information Between the robot and its environment.**

- - **The Robotics Institute of America(RIA) considers classes 3-6.** 

- - **The Association Rrancaise de Robotique combines classes 2. 3, and 4.**

**that is actuated by an operator.** 

**Class 2: Fixed sequence robot: A device that performs the successive Stages of a task according to a predetermined and fixed program.
